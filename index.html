Jubin Pakdel
Preprint, 2026
PDF

Giving JEPA and MAE identical prediction tasks on an active matter simulation shows latent prediction is more robust than pixel prediction: JEPA has lower error on 22 of 24 matched ablations, and MAE's worst case is 3× JEPA's.

Preprint, 2026
PDF

Adding a token reconstruction loss to the Compress and Attend Transformer recovers token identity lost to language-model-loss compression, improving linguistic probe scores and perplexity.
